
# BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War

## Introduction

**Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War (TYBW)** is a Japanese action and supernatural anime based on the manga written by *Tite Kubo*.

The story follows **Ichigo Kurosaki** and the Soul Reapers as they face a powerful new enemy known as the **Quincy Empire (Wandenreich)**, led by Yhwach.

## Main Characters

1. **Ichigo Kurosaki** - A Substitute Soul Reaper who protects his friends and the Soul Society.
2. **Rukia Kuchiki** - A Soul Reaper who helped Ichigo discover his powers.
3. **Byakuya Kuchiki** - Captain of the 6th Division of the Gotei 13.
4. **Kenpachi Zaraki** - Captain of the 11th Division who loves fighting.
5. **Genryusai Yamamoto** - Former Captain-Commander of the Gotei 13.
6. **Yhwach** - The powerful leader of the Quincy Empire.
